Dress Code

We invite our guests to join us in elegant evening attire for a celebration filled with timeless romance.

FOR HER

Floor-length gowns or elegant formal dresses in sophisticated, evening-appropriate styles.

FOR HIM

Tuxedos or dark formal suits paired with classic evening accessories.
A little note
As our celebration will take place in a garden setting, we recommend comfortable formal footwear for the outdoor ceremony. We kindly request that guests refrain from wearing white, ivory, or bridal-inspired attire. Think timeless elegance, soft neutrals, and classic evening glamour.

FAQ

What time should I arrive?

We kindly recommend arriving at least 20–30 minutes before the ceremony begins to allow time for seating.

We love your little ones. However, our wedding will be an adults-only celebration, with the exception of those specifically named on the invitation.

Yes! Our dress code is Black Tie Optional. Please refer to the Dress Code section for further details.

Our Love Story

Our story began on an ordinary autumn afternoon in New York City. Hannah was reaching for the last book on a little bookstore shelf when Adam,, standing just beside her, reached for the very same one. A brief laugh turned into a conversation, and a simple afternoon became the beginning of something neither of them had expected. What followed were countless coffee dates, long walks through the city, spontaneous weekend trips, and quiet evenings that somehow became their favorite memories. Over time, they discovered that love wasn’t always about grand gestures. Sometimes, it was about finding someone who made an ordinary Tuesday feel extraordinary. And then, on a beautiful autumn evening overlooking the Connecticut countryside, Adam asked Hannah the easiest question she had ever answered. She said yes. Now, surrounded by the people who mean the most to them, they are ready to begin their next chapter. And so, their forever begins.
